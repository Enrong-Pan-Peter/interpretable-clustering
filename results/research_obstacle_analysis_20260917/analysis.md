## Statistical fallacy scope: 11/11 checked

This is a scoped interpretation audit, not proof that every possible statistical bias is absent. No p-values, confidence intervals, causal estimates, or significance labels are generated.

1. **Simpson's paradox:** Stratified by dataset, representation, fixed bound, and readout; no pooled headline accuracy.
2. **Ecological fallacy:** Seed means describe procedures; no individual patient or clinical claims.
3. **Berkson's paradox:** Real-data contrasts and enriched gene universe are selected populations; generalization is limited to that scope.
4. **Collider bias:** No causal adjustment or claim; observational population filtering could induce dependencies and is not ruled out.
5. **Base-rate neglect:** Saved group counts and admissibility are checked; single-population controls have no external ARI.
6. **Regression to the mean:** Restarts are selected on discovery objectives; old-versus-new pilot differences are not a paired treatment effect.
7. **Survivorship bias:** Manifest coverage is checked and invalid-model denominators retained rather than silently treated as zero.
8. **Look-elsewhere effect:** All prescribed bounds/readouts are reported; no winner is chosen using test ARI and no significance test is performed.
9. **Garden of forking paths:** These are development ablations designed after an earlier pilot; no preregistration or confirmatory claim is implied.
10. **Correlation is not causation:** Label agreement and feature associations do not establish biological mechanisms or causality.
11. **Reverse causality:** No temporal or causal direction is asserted; it cannot be inferred from these data.
